JEPPIRAN En-route Development Dataset

This regional development dataset is derived from the public x-plane-navdata
dataset maintained at:
https://github.com/mcantsin/x-plane-navdata

Upstream data header:

http://github.com/mcantsin/x-plane-navdata

The upstream repository states that the X-Plane navigation data is published
under the GNU General Public License version 3 and includes global nav-aids,
fixes and airways.

JEPPIRAN subset:
- Geographic development window: latitude 24..41 N, longitude 43..64 E
- Source cycle: 2012.08
- Purpose: development/testing of the vector en-route renderer
- NOT FOR OPERATIONAL NAVIGATION

When replacing this development dataset, preserve the license and attribution
requirements of the replacement source.
